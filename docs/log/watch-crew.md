# Threat watch crew

The public feed check dispatches twin work after a successful response. The model-review stream also dispatches Scout on inventory matches and the crew handoff on a fresh report. Each matching item receives an approval-gated To-do. The floor follows one lead match per batch, prioritizing actual modeled controls or rules-engine path evidence. An active device-analysis run keeps the floor while watch work is recorded in the crew feed.

The Watch rows show **Detected → Proposed** and the To-do details show **From threat watch**. Mitigation effects are applied to a copy of the twin and scored against the current risk report. Procedural steps show zero risk change. FDA recall comparisons and simulated drills retain their labels. The watch summary and local resolution rules are deterministic. Provider recommendations keep their existing approval workflow.

Verify in the running app by opening Threat watch, choosing **Run now**, and checking the walking crew, Watch rows and the first To-dos. Run the watch cue, resolution, feed status, cache and To-do tests plus `npx tsc --noEmit`.
