# iPhone call demo

- A live **Describe device** capture used “Apple MacBook Pro laptop with Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Thunderbolt ports, brought in by a contractor” at **Auto**, which placed LT-901 in Filler 3. The results panel and escalation card agreed on **$290,000 estimated per event**. The rules engine cleared the corporate office placement, modeled at **$0 per event**. The plant risk meter showed **48/100, +2**. Replaying the captured device profile through the local rules engine gave **46.09 → 48.41 (+2.32)**. The alert fired on loss at the default **$100,000 / 60 / +8** settings. A recovery attempt also submitted the description and was canceled before a result was recorded.
- The paired phone reloads the current alert on foregrounding and fetches its authorized destination again. A trusted tap follows the native full-width `tel:` link. The desktop receipt records the tap. Apple documents the additional system confirmation in Safari. [Apple Phone Links](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/featuredarticles/iPhoneURLScheme_Reference/PhoneLinks/PhoneLinks.html)
- A Chromium test against the running app saved the test destination on the laptop, paired a second page, raised a labeled test alert while that page was hidden, and verified the dial link, tap receipt and approval on return. The test prevented native dialing. A physical iPhone call, an actual iOS lock/unlock and the photo capture remain unverified.
