# MacBook Pro on-call recording

Add `SLACK_WEBHOOK_URL=...` to the server’s `.env` to enable Slack alerts, including **Run test alert**.

1. **Set up the devices.** The presenter runs Pyre Witness on the laptop and keeps their iPhone nearby to receive the call. The collaborator holds a separate phone with voice service. Connect the laptop and collaborator’s phone to the same Wi-Fi and check that the presenter’s iPhone can receive calls.
2. **Pair the collaborator’s phone.** On the laptop, open the escalation bell and choose **Pair phone**. The collaborator scans the on-call QR, opens it in Safari on iPhone, and taps **Enable ringer**. Wait for **Phone paired** on the laptop. Keep the on-call tab available. Pairing links expire after 45 minutes.
3. **Save the destination.** On the laptop, open the bell and choose **Add your phone**. Enter the presenter’s name and their real iPhone number with the country code. Leave **Call this number when a threshold is crossed** unchecked for the paired-phone call, then choose **Save phone**.
4. **Capture the MacBook Pro.** Keep **Location: Auto** to start at Filler 3. Choose **Add a device → Phone QR**. The collaborator scans this second QR, photographs the MacBook Pro, taps **Send to laptop**, then returns to the on-call tab. A fresh QR capture starts analysis automatically. Alternatively, choose **Add a device → Upload**, select a photo, and tap **Analyze**.
5. **Show the alert and call.** Read the MacBook Pro alert’s per-event estimate and show its checked placement. The text rehearsal estimated **$290k per event** at Filler 3 and **$0** at the corporate office, with plant risk at **48/100** (**+2** displayed). The loss crossed the default **$100k** threshold. Use the photo run’s own figures. The collaborator unlocks their phone if needed, returns to the on-call tab, taps **Call [presenter’s name]**, and confirms the system prompt. The presenter answers on their iPhone. [Apple Phone Links](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/featuredarticles/iPhoneURLScheme_Reference/PhoneLinks/PhoneLinks.html)
6. **Approve the action.** After the call, the collaborator returns to the on-call tab and taps **Answer** to open the Chief briefing. Review the displayed twin update, tap **Approve action**, and show **Approved** and the updated twin on the laptop. If the alert offers only **Acknowledge**, record acknowledgment and describe the checked recommendation from the capture panel.
